# Visual Notes

- Use an institutional navy navigation rail, a light neutral workspace, white ledger surfaces, and a restrained bronze approval accent.
- Use a compact editorial page heading with a one-line current branch/account context.
- Prioritize transaction tables, amount alignment, approval state, and audit chronology. Reserve rounded outlines for buttons and form inputs.
- Keep success, waiting, failure, and stop states distinct through text, icon, and color. Color alone does not identify a state.
- On small screens, replace the wide table with stacked row summaries, preserve a persistent account/navigation affordance, and avoid hiding the spend limit.
- Use 120ms control feedback and 180ms panel transitions. Respect reduced motion.
