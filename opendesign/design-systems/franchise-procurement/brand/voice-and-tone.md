# Voice and Tone

- Write direct, neutral Korean for staff who need to decide or reconcile a purchase.
- Name the actor and action. Use sentences such as “본사에서 허용하지 않은 공급업체입니다.”
- Put the next actionable detail beside every status; identify whether an order is queued, blocked, simulated, or confirmed on chain.
- Format amounts with Korean grouping and an explicit `원` or `DKRW` suffix. Display date and time in Asia/Seoul.
- Use title case nowhere in Korean UI. Do not use emoji, slogans, or claims that a simulation is a real payment.
